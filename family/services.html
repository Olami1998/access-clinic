Services

Primary care and the visit you need today.

Colds, chronic illness, physicals, and minor injuries. For uninsured and self-pay patients, most services below are $69–$120, and sometimes less.

Primary and preventive care

  • Complete physical exam
  • Well-child exam
  • School and sports physical
  • Employment physical
  • Pre-operative physical
  • Immunizations, including flu shots
  • Diabetes
  • Hypertension
  • Asthma
  • EKG
  • Drug screens
  • Travel health
  • Sexual health, including STIs
  • Medical weight loss

Same-day concerns

  • Sore throat, cough, and fever
  • Earaches and sinus infections
  • Influenza and pneumonia
  • Urinary tract infections
  • Vomiting and diarrhea
  • Pink eye
  • Rash and allergic reactions
  • Allergies and asthma flares
  • Headaches and migraines
  • Minor cuts and lacerations
  • Minor burns
  • Sprains, strains, and bruises
  • Back and neck pain
  • Wound care and stitch removal
  • Incision and drainage
  • Acne

Medical weight loss

A supervised program with regular visits, a plan for cravings and appetite, and a maintenance phase. FDA-approved medication, including phentermine, is used only when it fits your health history.

Mental health

This clinic can start psychiatric care. Ongoing therapy and psychiatric treatment for ages 5 and older are at our sister practice, Access Behavioral Health, 410-744-1089.
