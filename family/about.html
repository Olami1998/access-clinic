About

A family clinic on Rolling Road.

Access Family Practice is family owned and family run. Patients come for primary care, urgent problems, and follow-up that stays in one place.

MT

Modupe Togun, DNP, FNP-BC, PMHNP

Medical officer of Access Family Practice and Walk-in Clinic. She is a board-certified family nurse practitioner and a psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner, so primary care and mental health treatment can start with the same clinician.

DNP means Doctor of Nursing Practice. FNP-BC is family practice board certification. PMHNP is certification in psychiatric mental health.

Who works here

The front desk and clinical staff speak English and Spanish. If you want the visit in Spanish, say so when you call or when you arrive. Información práctica en español.

The clinic also works with Access Behavioral Health for therapy and psychiatric care from age 5.
