Patients

Your first visit, and the ones after.

Walk in during open hours, or call 410-298-1931 if you want the registration packet before you arrive.

1

Call or walk in

New patients can arrive during office hours. Arriving a few minutes early leaves time for paperwork.

2

Bring these

Photo ID, insurance card, pharmacy name, and a list of medicines and allergies.

3

Ask for records

Email info@accessfamilyclinic.com or fax 410-298-1932 for forms and record requests.

Telehealth

Video visits are booked by phone. Staff confirm the time and send joining instructions. Join from a private place with your insurance card and medicine list ready.

If you cannot connect at the scheduled time, call the office rather than waiting on the line.

Guides at the office

Printed explanations are available for common topics. Ask at the visit.

  • Blood pressure and cholesterol
  • Diabetes and foot care
  • Weight management
  • Flu season
  • Child ear infections
  • Asthma and allergies
