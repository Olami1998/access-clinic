About

Primary care and mental health, side by side.

Access Family Practice and Access Behavioral Health use the same Rolling Road building, the same fax line, and the same clinical lead. They keep separate phones because the visits are different.

MT

Modupe Togun, DNP, FNP-BC, PMHNP

Medical officer of Access Family Practice. She is a board-certified family nurse practitioner and a psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner, which is why medical visits and psychiatric care can be coordinated instead of starting over with a stranger.

The family practice staff speak English and Spanish.

Which door

Fever, a physical, blood pressure, a sprain: family practice, and you can walk in.

Therapy, a psychiatric evaluation, ADHD, depression, or medication for a mental health condition: behavioral health, and you need an appointment.

The building

3110 N. Rolling Road, Windsor Mill, MD 21244. Family practice is Suite 101, across from Woodlawn Motor Coach and about six blocks north of Security Mall.

Behavioral health also lists 7502 Windsor Mill Road. Use that address only when the appointment says so.
