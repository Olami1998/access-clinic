Access Behavioral Health

Mental health care, by appointment.

Evaluation, therapy, and medication management for ages 5 and older. Call 410-744-1089 or toll-free 877-840-2965.

Common reasons to call

  • Anxiety or depression
  • ADHD
  • Mood disorders
  • Grief
  • OCD or PTSD
  • Psychosis, including schizophrenia
  • Personality disorders
  • Substance use with a mental health condition

First visit

The front desk schedules the assessment and sends intake forms: consent for treatment, the privacy notice, and permission to coordinate with your other clinicians.

Bring your insurance card, medicine list, allergies, and any referral. Give 48 hours’ notice to cancel.

New-patient details

Crisis

Call 911 for immediate danger. Call or text 988 for a mental health crisis. Do not use the appointment line as an emergency service.
