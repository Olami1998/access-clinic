How treatment starts

Each person gets an initial assessment. From there we write a plan for that person. It may be short-term therapy, longer individual therapy, group therapy including cognitive behavioral therapy, or ongoing medication management.

We take referrals from patients themselves, primary care, therapists, and the court system.
