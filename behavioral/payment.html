Payment

Know the cost before treatment starts.

Call your insurance plan and ask whether Access Behavioral Health is in network, and what your copay or deductible is. That call prevents a surprise bill.

Plans we contract with

Call your plan and confirm Access Behavioral Health is in network before the first visit.

  • Maryland Medicaid / Carelon
  • Medicare
  • Beacon
  • CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield
  • Wellpoint
  • Maryland Physicians Care
  • Priority Partners
  • UnitedHealthcare, including Medicaid
  • Aetna Maryland Medicaid
  • Aetna commercial and Medicare
  • MedStar Medicaid
  • Magellan

Private pay and out of network

You can pay privately. Ask the front desk for the current rate at 410-744-1089 or info@accessbehavioralclinic.com.

If we are not in network, we can give you a receipt to send to your plan for reimbursement.

Missed visits

Give 48 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Without that notice, the missed session is charged.
