New patients

The first appointment starts with a phone call.

Call 410-744-1089 or email info@accessbehavioralclinic.com. The front desk will set the visit and send the intake packet.

1

Call

Tell us the patient’s age, the reason for the visit, and the insurance plan.

2

Complete intake

The packet includes consent for treatment, the notice of privacy practices, and permission for your clinician to coordinate with the rest of your care team.

3

Come to the visit

Arrive at the office named in your appointment. Do not assume which of the two addresses is yours.

What to bring

  • Insurance card, plus any referral or authorization
  • List of medicines and doses
  • List of medicine allergies
  • Questions you want answered
  • Recent test results related to this concern
  • Photo ID
Cancellations

Please give 48 hours’ notice. A missed visit without that notice is charged for the session. The current fee schedule is available from the front desk.

If you are in crisis before your appointment, call or text 988 or call 911. Do not wait for the scheduled visit.
